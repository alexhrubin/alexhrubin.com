Hello there! 👋

I'm a PhD candidate in the Radulaski group at UC Davis, where I study quantum optics and quantum information.

My research focuses on light-matter interaction in solid-state systems, particularly color centers in silicon carbide. I’m also interested in using quantum computers to simulate and emulate open cavity QED.

Previously I’ve been various kinds of engineer (software/hardware/optical): at the Amazon's Center for Quantum Computing and PsiQuantum (among other places!).
